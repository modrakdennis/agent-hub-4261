## AI Tip

**Ask for the failure modes before you ask for the answer.** On any technical question — a Rev. Proc. application, an entity-structure choice, a basis calculation — add one line: *"Before answering, list the three ways a practitioner most often gets this wrong, then answer."* You get the same answer plus a built-in review checklist, and the model tends to be more careful about the actual answer because it has just enumerated the traps. It works especially well on new guidance like Rev. Proc. 2026-20, where the common errors are the whole risk.
