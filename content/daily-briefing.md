## AI Tip

**Ask for the critique before the draft.** When you want AI help on something with a right answer — a memo on a client's basis calculation, a position on a deduction — don't ask it to write the thing. Ask it to list the ways the position could be wrong, or what an examiner would push on first. You get a checklist you can actually test against the authority, instead of confident prose you now have to fact-check line by line. The order matters: critique first, draft second, and the draft comes out stronger because you already know where the soft spots are.
