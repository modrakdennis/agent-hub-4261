## Industry News

### IRS — e-News for Tax Professionals 2026-39 (Oct 1)

The heaviest item of the day, and most of it is practice-relevant.

- **New IRS mobile app replaces IRS2Go.** Clients can now check refund and amended-return status, view balances, make payments, view select notices and letters, pull transcripts, and retrieve an IP PIN. Initially it only exposes selected Individual Online Account features — Business Tax Account and Tax Pro Account stay on IRS.gov. Warn clients about look-alike apps. [Announcement](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-launches-new-mobile-app-expanding-digital-services-for-taxpayers) · [IRS app page](https://www.irs.gov/help/app)
- **Special per diem rates, effective Oct 1, 2026** — Notice 2026-60, including the transportation industry rate, incidental-expenses-only rate, and the high-low substantiation localities. [Notice 2026-60 (PDF)](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-26-60.pdf)
- **Form 8802 user fee increase, effective Oct 1, 2026** — U.S. residency certification goes from $85 to $105 for individuals and $185 to $230 for businesses. [Fact sheet](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/form-8802-application-for-united-states-residency-certification-user-fee-fact-sheet)
- **Proposed regs on §1062 farmland installment payments** — lets eligible sellers of qualifying farmland to qualified farmers spread the tax on the gain over four equal annual installments. Comments due **Nov 30, 2026**. [Press release](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/treasury-irs-issue-proposed-regulations-on-installment-payments-for-tax-on-certain-farmland-sales)
- **Notice 2026-62** — Treasury/IRS describe novel investment fund strategies that may produce results inconsistent with the underlying rules, and request comments. Useful read if any client is being pitched fund-level tax structuring. [PDF](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-26-62.pdf)
- **Rev. Rul. 2026-20** — treatment of a taxpayer who transfers appreciated securities to a newly formed ETF where some of those securities are later distributed to redeem another holder's shares. [PDF](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rr-26-20.pdf)
- **Notice 2026-58** — obsoletes 71 more pieces of IRB guidance (third round under the deregulation EOs). [PDF](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-26-58.pdf)
- **IRIS meetings** — Working Group Oct 14, 1–2 p.m. ET (software developers/transmitters); IRIS 101 Oct 28, 1–2 p.m. ET (overview and FIRE transition).
- **Family caregiver rules** — reminder that in-home caregivers for elderly or disabled relatives are generally employees, making the person cared for the employer with W-2 and possible employment-tax obligations. [Tax tip](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/caring-for-a-family-member-or-relative-understand-the-special-tax-rules)

## AI Tip

When you want a model to review a document rather than summarize it, give it the standard to review against *before* you give it the document — "Here is our WISP checklist; now read this engagement letter and flag every gap" works far better than pasting the letter first and asking what's wrong. Models anchor hard on whatever arrives first, so the criteria should arrive first.
