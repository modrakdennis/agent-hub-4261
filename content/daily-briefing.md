## AI Tip

**Make your AI show its work before it does the work.** On a research-heavy question — a technical tax position, a client memo, a thorny reconciliation — add this to your prompt: *"Before answering, list the sub-questions you need to resolve and the authority you'd cite for each. Then stop and let me confirm."*

You get a checkable outline instead of a confident paragraph, you catch a wrong framing in ten seconds rather than after reading 800 words, and when the answer does come it's anchored to sources you already approved. It's the single cheapest change you can make to how you prompt.
