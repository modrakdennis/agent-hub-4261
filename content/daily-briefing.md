## AI Tip

When you need a document summarized *for a specific decision*, don't ask for "a summary" — tell the model what you'll do with it. "Summarize this engagement letter" gets you a book report. "Read this engagement letter and list only the clauses that change my scope or my liability if the client's numbers turn out wrong" gets you the two paragraphs you actually needed. Same input, a fraction of the reading.
